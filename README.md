# Privacy Policy

**Quantum Screen Dimmer** and **Quantum Dimmer Pro Key**, by Quantum Eraser
Last updated: November 11, 2026

These apps do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or usage data.

## On your phone
- Neither app has internet access. Android blocks network access for apps that don't request it, so nothing can be sent from your device.
- Quantum Screen Dimmer uses Android's Accessibility API only to draw the dimming layer and the optional floating slider over your screen, and to detect quick volume-button combinations (Volume Down, Up, Down turns dimming off; Volume Up 3 times runs an optional shortcut). It does not read what is on your screen and does not record key presses.
- The sunset schedule uses a city you pick from a list built into the app, or coordinates you type yourself. The app never asks for or reads your location.
- Your settings (brightness, filter, presets, schedule) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. If you export a backup file, it is saved where you choose and the app never sends it anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes your settings.
- The Pro Key app has no permissions and stores nothing. The main app only checks whether it is installed.

## Purchases
- The Pro Key is sold through Google Play, which processes your payment under Google's own privacy policy. We receive only the limited order information Google Play gives every developer (such as an order number and the country of purchase) and use it only for accounting and refunds. We never see your payment details.

## Children
- The apps are not directed at children and collect no data from anyone.

## Changes
- If this policy changes, the new version will be published with a new date.
